# Day 28 Lab - Governance, Compliance & Adoption Metrics

**Level 3: Advanced | Group | 20 min | Dev and QA, different source metrics**

Template: `METRICS_OUTLINE.template.md`.

1. Draft a lightweight adoption / ROI metrics outline for your own team (suggestions accepted, time saved, defect trend, and so on).
2. Find one metric in your own draft that could be gamed.
3. Propose a guardrail for it.

Pair every activity metric with a quality or outcome check. Acceptance rate alone is not enough: accepting more is not accepting better.

## Assignment

Your team's draft metrics outline.

## Assessment: metrics-framework review. At least one outcome metric, plus a named gameable metric and its guardrail.
