# Day 29 Lab - Train-the-Trainer: Coaching Others

**Level 3: Advanced | Paired | 20 min | Dev and QA variants**

Plan: `MINI_LESSON.template.md`.

1. Prepare a 10-minute mini-lesson on ONE Copilot technique. Dev teaches a Dev technique, QA teaches a QA technique.
2. Deliver it to a partner who does not know that technique.
3. Collect one piece of feedback, and ask your partner to repeat the idea back. That is the real test of clarity.

Teach one technique deeply rather than three shallowly.

## Assignment

The mini-lesson, refined with your partner's feedback, ahead of the capstone.

## Assessment: peer-rated mini-lesson (clarity, accuracy, engagement)
