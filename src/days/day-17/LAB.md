# Day 17 Lab - IDE Customization, Extensions & Keybindings

**Level 2: Intermediate | Individual | 20 min | Dev and QA, no split**

Samples: `keybindings.sample.json` (VS Code), `snippets.sample.json`. Adapt to your IDE (JetBrains: Settings > Keymap).

1. Rebind "accept suggestion" and "open Chat" to keys you will actually use.
2. Create one snippet for the Copilot action you use most (for example `/tests`).
3. Share your keybinding with a partner and try theirs.

Pick the action you do MOST OFTEN, not the flashiest one.

## Assignment

Use one new keybinding or snippet in daily work for the rest of the week.

## Assessment: live setup demo, 2 min each (works, faster than the default, you can say why you chose it)

Light day by design: use spare time to catch up on Days 11-16.
