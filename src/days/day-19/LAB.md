# Day 19 Lab - Copilot on Shared / Cross-Team Codebases

**Level 2: Intermediate | Individual | 20 min | Dev and QA variants**

Shared module: `shared/inventory.js` (+ test). Rules: `STYLE_GUIDE.md`. Checker: `node src/days/day-19/check-style.js`.

1. Make a small change with Copilot (for example `removeItem(inventory, sku)` with a test).
2. Check EVERY suggestion against the style guide, not only for correctness. `draft-change.sample.txt` shows what drift looks like.
3. Find and correct at least one suggestion that broke a rule, and name the rule.

## Assignment

One real style-conformant change in a shared / cross-team repo.
