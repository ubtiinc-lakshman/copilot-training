# Announcement draft (send Mon Oct 26)

Subject: Change to the GitHub Copilot Training: you will build a real app every week

Hi all,

From tomorrow the training changes. The goal is simple: you learn Copilot by using it to build something real, and you finish with working apps and the confidence to do it again at work.

**New weekly rhythm**

| Day | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Tue, Wed, Thu | Conference room (in person) | The skills, tools and connectors you need to build an app, with a hands-on lab |
| Fri | Online, live call, 90 min | Build day. We build an app live and you build yours. Glen and Naveen help |
| Mon | Online, live call, 90 min | You show your deployed app and tell us the challenges you hit and how you solved them |

The program still ends **Tue Dec 1**.

**What you build:** every Thursday you click "Give me my pain point" on the training site and get a real-world problem (a common person's problem, or one inside an organisation). It is picked at random, and no two people get the same one, and nobody gets the same one twice. You build an app for it by Monday, deploy it, and submit the link. That is five apps over five weeks.

**What we will use:** Next.js, Supabase, Vercel and GitHub Copilot. [Cloudflare may replace Vercel if there is a problem. We will confirm by Wed.]

**Before Wednesday please have**
- Node.js 22.12 or newer, and Git
- A GitHub account with Copilot working (done on Day 2)
- [Vercel and Supabase accounts: details to follow after we confirm account setup]

**How this is assessed**
- Each Monday: your deployed link works, your data is saved, there are no secrets in your code, and you can explain what was hard and how you fixed it
- To be certified: at least 80% attendance, at least 4 of 5 apps passing, and a quiz average of 70% or more
- The short quiz after each session still counts

**Everything else** (the earlier Copilot lessons) stays on the training site for you to learn at your own pace.
